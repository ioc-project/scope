CELESTIA CITY GUIDE
===================

ATTRACTIONS:
------------

1. Crystal Tower Observatory
   - Opening hours: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM
   - Admission: $25 per person
   - Description: A 300-meter glass tower offering panoramic views of the entire city

2. Starlight Gardens
   - Opening hours: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM
   - Admission: Free
   - Description: Botanical gardens with bioluminescent plants that glow at night

3. Museum of Celestial Arts
   - Opening hours: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
   - Admission: $18 per person
   - Description: Houses the world's largest collection of sky-themed artwork

4. Azure Lake Promenade
   - Open 24/7
   - Admission: Free
   - Description: Beautiful lakeside walkway with restaurants and shops

5. Moonstone Market
   - Opening hours: 11:00 AM - 11:00 PM
   - Admission: Free
   - Description: Historic marketplace featuring local crafts and international cuisine


SUGGESTED ITINERARY:
--------------------

DAY 1: Morning to Evening Tour

9:00 AM - 10:30 AM: Crystal Tower Observatory
- Travel time from city center: 15 minutes by metro
- Metro fare: $3.50
- Duration of visit: 1.5 hours
- Cost: $25

11:00 AM - 12:30 PM: Museum of Celestial Arts
- Travel time from Crystal Tower: 20 minutes by bus
- Bus fare: $2.50
- Duration of visit: 1.5 hours
- Cost: $18

12:30 PM - 2:00 PM: Lunch at Moonstone Market
- Travel time from Museum: 10 minutes walking
- Average meal cost: $15-25
- Duration: 1.5 hours

2:30 PM - 4:30 PM: Starlight Gardens
- Travel time from Moonstone Market: 25 minutes by tram
- Tram fare: $3.00
- Duration of visit: 2 hours
- Cost: Free

5:00 PM - 7:00 PM: Azure Lake Promenade
- Travel time from Starlight Gardens: 15 minutes by water taxi
- Water taxi fare: $8.00
- Duration of visit: 2 hours
- Cost: Free
- Dinner options available along promenade ($20-40)

TOTAL ESTIMATED COSTS:
- Admissions: $43
- Transportation: $32
- Meals: $35-65
- Grand Total: $110-140 per person
