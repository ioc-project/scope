VERDANTIA CITY GUIDE
====================

ATTRACTIONS:
------------

1. Emerald Canyon Bridge
   - Opening hours: 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM
   - Admission: $12 per person
   - Description: Suspended bridge over a 500-foot deep canyon with waterfalls

2. The Living Library
   - Opening hours: 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM
   - Admission: $15 per person
   - Description: A library built around living trees, with books and nature merged

3. Rainforest Canopy Walk
   - Opening hours: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM
   - Admission: $30 per person (includes guided tour)
   - Description: Elevated walkways through ancient rainforest canopy

4. Verdant Falls Water Park
   - Opening hours: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM
   - Admission: $45 per person
   - Description: Natural water park featuring actual waterfalls and pools

5. Jade Mountain Tea House
   - Opening hours: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM
   - Admission: Free entry (purchases required)
   - Description: Traditional tea house on mountainside with city views


SUGGESTED ITINERARY:
--------------------

DAY 1: Nature Adventure Tour

7:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Emerald Canyon Bridge
- Travel time from city center: 30 minutes by cable car
- Cable car fare: $8.00 round trip
- Duration of visit: 2 hours
- Cost: $12

9:30 AM - 11:30 AM: Jade Mountain Tea House (Breakfast)
- Travel time from Canyon Bridge: 10 minutes walking uphill
- Average breakfast cost: $12-18
- Duration: 2 hours
- Admission: Free

12:00 PM - 3:00 PM: Rainforest Canopy Walk
- Travel time from Tea House: 40 minutes by forest shuttle
- Shuttle fare: $6.00
- Duration of visit: 3 hours (includes guided tour)
- Cost: $30

3:30 PM - 5:30 PM: The Living Library
- Travel time from Canopy Walk: 35 minutes by eco-bus
- Eco-bus fare: $4.50
- Duration of visit: 2 hours
- Cost: $15

6:00 PM - 9:00 PM: Verdant Falls Water Park (Evening Session)
- Travel time from Library: 20 minutes by local train
- Train fare: $3.50
- Duration of visit: 3 hours
- Cost: $45
- Dinner available at park ($18-30)

TOTAL ESTIMATED COSTS:
- Admissions: $102
- Transportation: $22
- Meals: $30-48
- Grand Total: $154-172 per person
